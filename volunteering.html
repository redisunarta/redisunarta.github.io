Universitas Indonesia · Volunteering · 2016–2018

Volunteering

Three student programmes alongside my economics degree — two of them community development run by BEM UI and BEM FEB UI, the student executive bodies of the university and its Faculty of Economics and Business.

Devout FEB UI

BEM FEB UI · Sep 2016 – Apr 2018 · Pasir Putih, Depok

BEM FEB UI’s community development programme, focused on education and economic issues in Pasir Putih.

  • Built a library for elementary school children and ran monthly additional tutoring.
  • Helped local mothers start and run businesses suited to their comparative advantage.
  • Provided free financial literacy and health services once a year.
Desa Binaan BEM UI

Social Affairs, BEM UI · Feb 2017 – Sep 2017 · Bojong Pondok Terong, Citayam, Depok

BEM UI’s community development programme, covering the economy, infrastructure, education and health in Kelurahan Bojong Pondok Terong.

  • Ran training for housewives and school children — digital marketing, cooking, and the principles of healthy living, among others.
  • Worked with local youth organisations and other community stakeholders to deliver the programme.

Citayam is also where I later ran the field survey for my financial inclusion project.

Himakara UI

Karawang student community · Dec 2016 – Jan 2018 · Karawang Regency, West Java

The Universitas Indonesia community for students from Karawang Regency. Its main annual programme helps high-school students from Karawang understand the university entrance process.

  • Seminars and exhibitions on studying at Universitas Indonesia.
  • Campus tours for prospective students.
  • SIMAK UI try-out exams, the university’s own entrance test.
